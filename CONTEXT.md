# arslanrehmani.com — Project Context

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

## Who this is for

Arslan Rehmani builds operational systems and AI agents that replace manual work. This site is his personal brand. The long-term aim is to be the name businesses think of for agentic systems that solve operational and finance problems, the way one well-known name owns SEO.

## Positioning

The site talks about the problems Arslan solves. It does not talk about him.

- **Say:** the business problem, what was built, what changed, in the client's numbers.
- **Do not say:** his age, where he is based, his qualifications, his courses, his tech stack, his tools, or how hard he works.
- **Tone:** qualified, experienced and calm. A London or New York operator should read it and take him seriously before the first call. Confidence comes from specifics, never from adjectives.
- **Voice:** first person singular ("I"). Never "we".

Finance and business competence is shown through the problems chosen (month-end close, reconciliation, stock against ledger), not by naming a credential.

## Not on the site yet

- **Vanthrope.** It will become the agency. It is not mentioned anywhere until Arslan says so.
- **ACCA, courses, certificates, the Proficient AI Engineer directory.** None are named on the site.
- **Location.** Not stated.

## Case studies and products are different things

**Case studies** are client engagements with the client's real figures. They live in `lib/content.ts` (`caseStudies`) and render on `/work`.

1. **TextileMode** — textile manufacturer. Always first.
   - 27 looms run from one system
   - 5 modules, 11 management reports
   - 5 back-office roles replaced (the figure in the signed engagement; this is the number to use)
   - 40+ hours of manual work removed every week
   - Live for 10+ months on real financial data
2. **Belhide** — a client's multi-channel ecommerce brand. Always a client, never Arslan's own business.
   - One platform connecting Shopify, Amazon, Google Analytics and Search Console
   - Daily AI briefing
   - Automation across channels

**Products** are things a visitor can open and use. They live in `lib/content.ts` (`products`) and render on `/products`. The current four:

| Product | Problem | Public URL |
|---|---|---|
| LoomWorks | A factory floor run on registers and spreadsheets | None yet: `loomworks.arslanrehmani.com` does not resolve |
| BrandPilot | Every sales channel in a different dashboard | brandpilot.arslanrehmani.com |
| MarketLens | Research that takes an analyst hours | marketlens.arslanrehmani.com |
| Legality AI | Standard agreements that wait days for a draft | legality.arslanrehmani.com |

Course builds and experiments are ingredients inside products. They are never listed as separate portfolio items.

## Client systems are not public

`erp.textilemode.com` and `erp.belhide.com` did not resolve when checked on 17 November 2026. The site does not link to them. It says the systems hold live client data and are walked through on a call.

## Target visitor

An operations director, finance lead or founder of a manufacturing or ecommerce business. Time-poor and skeptical of AI hype. They decide in 10 to 15 seconds whether to keep reading. They care about their problem and whether this person can solve it.

## What success looks like

A prospect arrives from a proposal or a cold email, recognises their own problem in the first screen, reads one case study with specific numbers, and starts a conversation. The site's job is that one moment.

## Timeline

The site is live but has almost no traffic today. It needs to be ready for real use by the new year, and it will carry weight from around March or April 2027.
